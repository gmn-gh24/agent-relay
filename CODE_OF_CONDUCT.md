# Code of Conduct

agent-relay is a small project. These rules keep it a place where people can report problems, ask questions and
send changes without being treated badly.

## What we expect

- Be civil. Argue with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Keep to the subject of the issue or pull request.
- Accept a "no" on scope. The maintainer decides what the project does and does not do.
- Respect other people's time: say what you tried, what happened and what you expected.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's identity.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or content unrelated to the project.
- Anything illegal, or aimed at harming another person's machines or accounts.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, commit messages and any other space run under this repository.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the repository. The
maintainer may do this without warning when the behavior is serious.

To report a problem, open a private report through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability"),
or, for something that is not a security matter, contact the maintainer through the profile at
<https://github.com/gmn-gh24>. Reports are handled in confidence, and the maintainer will not reveal who made one.
