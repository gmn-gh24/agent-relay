# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in agent-relay to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, background,
identity, experience or way of working. We act in ways that keep the community open and useful.

## Standards

Good behavior:

- Be civil and respectful. Argue with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Own your mistakes and fix them.
- Keep to the subject of the issue or pull request, and respect other people's time: say what you tried, what
  happened and what you expected.
- Accept a "no" on scope. The maintainer decides what the project does and does not do.

Not accepted:

- Insults, trolling, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private, and unwelcome sexual language or advances.
- Discrimination, or demeaning comments about a person's identity.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or content unrelated to the project.
- Anything illegal, or aimed at harming another person's machines or accounts.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, comments, commit messages and every other space run under
this repository, and to anyone who speaks for the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the repository. Serious
behavior can be acted on without warning. For other cases the usual steps are:

1. **Correction:** a private or public note that says what was wrong and why.
2. **Warning:** a note that further breaches will have consequences.
3. **Temporary block:** no contact in the project's spaces for a set time.
4. **Permanent block.**

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening a private report through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a
vulnerability**), or for something that is not a security matter, contact the maintainer through the profile at
<https://github.com/gmn-gh24>. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, handled in confidence, and the maintainer
will not reveal who made one.
